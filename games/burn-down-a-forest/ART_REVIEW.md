# Art review 1: fresh-eyes critic, 2026-11-09 (first-playable build)

Reviewed all 19 screenshots from `tests/screenshots.gd` against the studio's "Look and feel" standard. The next art pass works from this list. Mark each item done here when fixed, then get a second review.

## Problems, worst first

1. **Emoji are the icon set** (HUD, notebook, offer tag, popup). 🔥 is the currency, four tool icons and every label's bullet; ⬆️ ↔️ 💎 📺 ⚙️ are stock glyphs. Fix: one SVG icon set (2px ink line, two flat fills, a slight wobble). Draw each tool as the real object; embers as a cracked, glowing coal.
   - **Done.** 41 SVG icons drawn by `art/icons/make_icons.py` (2px ink line, two fills, seeded wobble) in `ui/icons/`; the coal is the currency, every tool is the real object; critters are Blender models now; the emoji font and its tool are gone and no emoji is left in code or UI text.
2. **One template panel everywhere**: dark-brown rounded rectangle, one corner radius, thin orange stroke, soft orange glow, glowing orange pill buttons. Fix: no glows at all. HUD pieces as charred planks with uneven cut ends and a hard 2px offset ink shadow.
   - **Done.** HUD pieces are drawn charred planks (`UiTheme.PlankBox`: saw-cut ragged ends, grain, fire-darkened ends, hard 3-4px ink shadow), transient things are paper and tags; no glow in the UI or the 3D view (bloom off).
3. **The "notebook" is a dark settings page** (sidebar nav, list rows, a Close button). Fix: cream paper #EFE3C8 with grain and ruled lines, scorched edges, a stitched spine, coloured index tabs. Each item gets a sketch and a price on a taped tag. Buy with an ink stamp; close with a ribbon.
   - **Done.** Leather cover, two #EFE3C8 pages with grain, ruled lines and scorched edges, a stitched spine, six coloured index tabs, a sketch per entry, prices on taped manila tags, ink stamps to buy (they thump), a red ribbon bookmark to close; the left page adds a doodled map of the rings.
4. **Type**: Outfit is the banned default sans. The script font is hairline and grey on brown, too thin to read on a phone, and its K reads as a stray capital ("NotebooK").
   - **Done.** IM Fell English SC (titles, names, prices), Alegreya Sans (body; lining tabular figures for the HUD counter, since IM Fell's old-style figures read "1.00M" as "I.ooM"), Caveat Brush at 22px+ in blue-black ink on paper. Outfit and Nothing You Could Do removed; OFL licences included.
5. **Occluding trees fade with a dotted dither pattern** that looks like a render bug. Fix: a soft circular cutout, or fade the canopy to an outlined silhouette.
   - **Done.** A round window is cut around the keeper with an inked rim where it meets each tree (no dither, no transparency). Tried outline-only silhouettes first; in dense oak woods they became a scribble, so the cutout won. The aim ray now skips the cut-away trees.
6. **Primitive models**: pines are stacked cones, oaks are faceted spheres, all one size, evenly scattered, flat-shaded. The hero is a sphere on a cone about 40px tall, hidden under the target label. Fix: Blender trees with crooked trunks, hooked tips, drooping tiers, knothole eyes and baked AO, in three sizes, plus hand-placed landmark trees. Hero 1.5-2x bigger with a tall hood, a lantern and a rim light; the label above the ring.
   - **Done.** Every model is a Blender script in `art/blender/` with ray-cast AO, gradients and rim highlights baked into vertex colours: pines and oaks in three sizes (crooked trunks, drooping star tiers and hooked tips, scalloped canopies with hanging points, knothole eyes), two giants, the Elder Tree, six hand-placed landmark trees. The keeper is 1.6x bigger, with a tall flopping hood, a brass lantern and a shader rim light, framed over the shoulder; the target label sits above the crosshair, clear of him.
7. **Fire blows out to pure white**, and flames are flat fans of cards. Fix: core #FFC15E, body #E8642C, smoky tips, hand-drawn animated flames with an ink edge.
   - **Done.** Cel flames in `shaders/flame.gdshader`: a #E8642C body, a #FFC15E core, a darker band at the edge, smoky tips, an ink outline, redrawn as held drawings at 8 fps. Opaque with a cut-out edge, linear tonemap, no bloom: never white. Burning trees glow in creeping ember veins.
8. **The spawn looks like a blockout**: two pastel boxes cut off by the frame, z-fighting seams, randomly placed crystals, and the bottom 40% of the view is empty brown fog. Fix: cottages half-swallowed by roots, warm lit windows, a path into the forest.
   - **Done.** Four round cottages with roots over their roofs and warm lit windows frame the spawn view like stage wings. A cobbled square has a well, firewood, fences, lantern posts, crawling roots and gardens. A winding path leads into the woods under a root arch between two landmark pines. The crystals are gone.
9. **No value structure in the palette**: violet oaks and teal pines are the same lightness; cyan sparkles are sprinkled evenly everywhere. Fix: the palette below, sparkles only on cursed hearts.
   - **Done.** The palette is in the art bible with measured L* values: plum 14, violet 32, teal 36, sage 67, fire core 82, mint 89. Mint appears only on cursed knots and hearts, and the only sparkles drift round the giants' and the Elder Tree's hearts. Plum fog lifts the distance, and the ambient light warms as the meadow spreads.
10. **The meadow** is one star-shaped flower scattered evenly over saturated green with hard grid edges. Fix: 3-4 species in clusters that thin out at the edges, muted green, grass tufts.
    - **Done.** Poppy, oxeye daisy, buttercup and harebell grow in drifts of one species, bunched in each cell and thinning toward trails, rocks and water, with sage grass tufts everywhere. The ground is muted sage, and a domain-warped cell lookup removes the grid edges.
11. **The tutorial** is a centred box with PC instructions, and the offer tag says "Press B". Fix: phone-only lines of about six words beside their control, with a drawn finger.
    - **Done.** Seven hints of about six words each ("Hold to light the cursed grass", "Thumb here to walk") on taped paper in the keeper's hand, placed beside the burn button, stick, notebook, counter or wind sign. On phones a drawn finger presses or swipes. The offer tag says "tap (or click) to watch a short ad"; no key names.
12. **Copy and controls**: exclamation marks ("Burning!"), Title Case, middle dots everywhere, all-caps "HOLD TO BURN", a stock joystick, a centred popup with a gift-emoji pill button. Fix: sentence case, no "!", a branded wooden burn button, the starter pack as a parcel tied with twine.
    - **Done.** All copy is sentence case, with no "!" and no middle dots. The burn button is a log slice with a flame branded into it (lit ember only while burning); jump is a slice with a chevron; the stick is a carved wooden ring. The starter pack is a brown paper parcel tied with twine, with a label tag, an ink stamp and a handwritten "not now", set off-centre.

## Keep

1. The cold cursed forest turning into a warm meadow, and the glowing cracked-ember ash. **Kept** (and the ambient light now warms with it).
2. The dry writing voice: "Ash. Flowers soon.", "Click, whoosh.", "a very polite dragon." **Kept**, and new lines follow it.
3. The notebook as the shop, with handwritten margin notes; the hazy depth in the grove view. **Kept** (plum fog; the notes are in the keeper's blue-black hand).

## Art direction for the next pass

- **Palette:** shadows plum #2B1F3A, oaks violet #5A3F7A, pines slate teal #2F5C66, all under 40% lightness. Mint #9FF0D0 only on cursed knots. Fire is the only warm light: #E8642C and #FFC15E, never white. Meadow sage #8FAE5E with cream and poppy #D9483B petals.
- **UI:** paper #EFE3C8, ink #2E1E14, char #1A1310; ember orange only on the main action. Motif: a field notebook plus burnt-wood signs (grain, scorch marks, tape, tags on string, hard ink shadows).
- **Type:** IM Fell English SC for titles and numbers, Alegreya Sans for body, Caveat Brush at 22px or larger for notes (all open-licensed; include the licence files).
- **Shapes:** a linocut storybook. Cursed things are spiky and crooked; the hero, village and meadow are round.
- **Lighting:** cool moonlight from the upper left, real point lights on the fire, a rim light on the hero, ambient light warming as the meadow grows.
- **Inspirations:** linocut picture books, Scandinavian folk painting, old forestry signs.

Next: a second fresh-eyes review of the new screenshots and the contact sheet (the art pass is written up in NOTES.md, "Art bible").

# Art review 2: second fresh-eyes critic, 2026-11-09 (after the art pass)

**Verdict: not yet.** The 2D layer (planks, notebook, icons, copy) passes as hand-made. The 3D world fills most of every frame, and its sticker fire, gnome-like hero and blob trees still read as hobby or AI work. The next pass is about the 3D.

## Problems, worst first

1. **Fire looks like emoji stickers**: every flame is the same orange-yellow teardrop card, upright and evenly spaced like birthday candles, jagged edges, no smoke, lights nothing; burning canopies get a generic noise "lava camo" texture. Fix: inked flipbook flames that vary in size and lean with the wind, one tall leading flame per cluster, plum smoke, sparks, a warm glow cast on the ground; charring as glowing cracks creeping along the model.
   - **Done.** An inked flipbook (`art/fx/make_fx.py` → `fx/flames.png`: 3 flame silhouettes plus a tall leading flame, 4 held drawings each, carved lines, sooty tips) in clusters that vary in size and position and lean harder the taller they are, one leading flame per tree or grass crest, sparks in the same draw call, cel-shaded plum smoke puffs; a blurred per-cell fire-light map casts warm light on ground, trunks and props (phones too); burning trees char from the roots up behind a thin ember line with thin glowing cracks on bark only.
2. **Keeper and critters are primitive builds**: sphere head with diamond eyes, blush dots and a ball nose on a cone body with a belt and a white fur hem reads as Santa or a garden gnome; the spiky hat breaks the "hero is round" rule. Rabbit is a snowman, hedgehog a ball, sprite a flat kite, tools are boxes and cylinders. Fix: redesign the keeper around fire (soot-stained cape, ember scarf, round hood, lantern on a pole, boots), sculpted with bevels and painted vertex colour; critters with real heads and legs; tools with bevels and wear.
   - **Done.** Keeper rebuilt round (`characters.py`): bell cape gone black and ragged at the hem with an ochre patch stitched on the back, fat knitted ember scarf with a tail that streams out when walking, round hood with a soft drooping tip over a soot-dark face with two warm eyes, chunky boots, mittens, brass lantern swinging from a crooked pole; edge wear, crease grime and painted mottle baked in. Rabbit, fox and hedgehog have real heads, snouts, eyes with glints and legs; the sprite is a plump flame with a curled tip; tools are bevelled and worn.
3. **Muddy values, lighting without intent**: one mid-dark plum/teal soup, no moonlight direction, no rim; the keeper disappears; the mint accent is almost absent. Fix: a key light with cast shadows, a cool rim on canopy tops, a darker foreground, mint knothole eyes on every cursed tree, a warm lantern pool under the keeper.
   - **Done.** The moon is now a strong key from the upper left with cast shadows (trees, cottages, keeper) over low plum ambient; a cold rim on canopy tops; the foreground nearer than the keeper is darkened; every pine and oak has mint knothole eyes; the keeper carries an inked outline, a moon rim and a warm lantern pool painted on the ground.
4. **Camera buried in foliage**: frames fill with purple blobs and the hero is hidden. Fix: camera collision, a clean radial fade of anything blocking the view, a steeper angle in the dense rings.
   - **Done.** The camera rises over any canopy at its spot (never inside one), tilts down 0.3 rad more in thick woods (still draggable), and everything up to the keeper's depth inside a screen-space circle round them is cut away with an inked rim (grass and bushes spared). The hero is always framed.
5. **Hex oaks are stock icosphere-cluster trees**, brighter than the #5A3F7A swatch; the Elder is just a bigger oak and shows in-game as a black trunk. Fix: scalloped canopies with hanging points, clawed branches, carved-line highlights, less saturation; the Elder gets its own landmark silhouette visible from the village.
   - **Done.** Oaks rebuilt from carved cushions (`forest_lib.Model.cushion`): lumpy domes cut into scallops that droop into hanging points, carved highlight lines following the scallops, clawed twigs, forked crooked trunks with clawed roots; vertex colours set below the swatch so moonlit tops land on #5A3F7A. The Elder is now a crown of five crooked finger limbs with clawed tips and weeping curse moss, ~28 m tall: the first thing a fresh game shows, over the woods from the village.
6. **Meadow looks like a paint-bucket fill**: lime not sage, hard edge against the stones, evenly scattered flowers. Fix: value variation, a ragged edge into the ash, drifts by species, a blooming animation.
   - **Done.** The meadow is painted by the ground shader from a mask: sage in lighter and darker drifts, a ragged noise-cut edge with a darker turf rim into the ash, damped against the moon so it sits mid-value; flowers grow in single-species drifts with bare grassy gaps; each flower's stem shoots up and then its head opens with an overshoot.
7. **Voronoi flagstone ground** reads as a procedural shader. Fix: hand-laid stones of varied sizes, a worn path into the woods, moss.
   - **Done.** The Voronoi shader is gone. Six hand-cut flagstone models (irregular, bevelled, worn on top, moss in the edges) are laid by a placer: a paved yard round the well, a doorstep at the lodge, stepping stones that thin out along the worn path into the woods; mossy patches in the packed earth.
8. **Village is symmetric and blank**: two near-identical huts flank a centred keeper; untextured wall slabs, smooth thatch, a perfect pyramid of identical logs. Fix: asymmetric layout, huts at different sizes, a landmark with chimney smoke, carved thatch and wear.
   - **Done.** Lopsided layout: a big round two-storey lodge as the landmark (tall leaning chimney with a smoke plume, lantern by the door, roots round its drum), a small leaning hut near right, a cottage further back, a woodshed; carved layered thatch (each band jutting at its lip, ragged eaves), damp and dirt at the plaster's foot, edge wear; the woodpile is uneven with a split log, a stray log and a chopping block with the axe in it.
9. **Emoji-like glyphs**: red floating hearts (the bible says mint), smiling flame and dragon icons, the stock pointing-hand cursor, an odd hand-rabbit icon on the critter toast. Fix: redraw in the game's ink icon style.
   - **Done.** Mint hearts with carved lines; the sprite without a grin; the dragon drawn as the felt puppet side on; a running hare; the pointer is a spent match and turns into a lit match over anything you can press (replaces the stock arrow and hand).
10. **The notebook is a shop-list template with a paper skin**: every row icon/title/blurb/price plus a second stamp button; the left page is the same on every tab; the Free magic page widens and clips the tabs; locked items use grey stamps like disabled web buttons. Fix: stamp the price tag itself, a different left-page sketch per tab, margin notes, locked items as pencil outlines, fix the width jump.
   - **Done.** The price tag is the button: tap it and the ink stamp thumps down on the tag ("in hand", "kept", "full", "yours"); no second button. Each chapter has its own left-page sketch and note (ring map in Tools, bellows with your levels, your helpers, what magic is running, the parcel, the dial); handwritten margin notes ("next!", "save up", "a fair price", "on now"); tools out of reach are pencil sketches with a dashed outline tag; titles wrap, so no chapter changes width.
11. **The ending is a card over gameplay** with the HUD still showing "Pinewood 17% burned". Fix: hide the HUD and sweep the camera over the restored valley at dawn. Phone screens say "click" instead of "tap".
   - **Done.** Burning the Elder hides the HUD, the rest of the cursed forest withers in a wave out from the heart and blooms (trails green over too), night turns to dawn and the camera sweeps up and round over the restored valley before the letter is set down; a finished forest stays at dawn. The dawn air clears and the hill woods are carved cushion oaks too, so the sweep doesn't land on flat lollipops in fog. Every phone string says tap.

## Keep

- The wood-and-paper UI: charred planks with saw-cut ends and hard shadows, taped handwritten notes, the offer tag on a string with a rope timer, the log-slice touch buttons. **Kept** unchanged.
- The notebook's craft and the consistent hand-drawn ink icons. **Kept**; the new icons follow the same 2 px ink, two-fill, wobble style.
- The dry, in-world, sentence-case copy. **Kept**; new lines follow it ("a fair price", "nothing here you can't burn for").
- Best models: the hooked cursed pines, the daisies, the root arch. **Kept**; the pines only gained mint eyes and a lighter bake.

# Art review 3: third fresh-eyes critic, 2026-11-09 (after review-2 fixes + QA)

**Verdict: Almost.** The UI and palette look made by people; the keeper, the fire at scale, the meadow and the ending (on screen most) still look like an early build. **Phone readability:** the village and notebook read well; the forest interior is too dark, HUD second lines and floating numbers are too small, and late-game fire walls hide the keeper. This review is the polish list toward "ready to play".

## Problems, worst first

1. **Fire at scale**: dozens of copies of the same three-prong flame card, upright, same size, no smoke; near the camera they become giant flat cutouts that bury the keeper and HUD; canopies next to the fire stay cold purple; ash is an even orange-dot speckle. Fix: one composed bonfire per tree (a leading flame plus a few licks), fewer cards and a fade near the camera, wind lean, a smoke plume, fire light tinting nearby canopies, a creeping glowing edge instead of the speckle.
   - **Done.** Each burning tree is one composed bonfire drawing (new flipbook rows: a leading tongue off to one side and licks stepping down, mirrored and boiled), set on the near side of its crown, with a lick at the roots on some; grass burns as a low ragged front with a crest here and there; under two cards per burning cell (was 4-9). Flames shrink away near the camera and are capped at a quarter of the screen's height, the whole card shears downwind, three-puff plumes bend away downwind with a warm base, a wider blur of the fire map lights canopy undersides, and the ground shows a thin glowing contour that creeps with the front (the speckle is gone; burnt ground is char, not black).
2. **The keeper**: a bell, a sphere hood and a black ball with two yellow dot eyes (the stock faceless-hood look); the "hand" is a pot, the head ~120 triangles; from behind it reads as a red bowling pin with a pale selection-style outline. Fix: one sculpted mesh with a floppy hood tip, shoulder cape, a satchel and notebook on the back, mittens, boots, swinging scarf tails; an ink outline only when the keeper is hidden.
   - **Done.** One subdivided Blender mesh (6K tris): rust cape with folds and a sooty ragged hem, mustard capelet, hood with a long floppy tip and tassel round a real face (nose, cheeks, eyes with glints), knitted scarf knotted at the back, satchel with the notebook strapped on; knitted mittens, laced boots and two swinging scarf ends move on their own. The ink outline only fades in when a fire stands between the camera and the keeper; the rim light is soft.
3. **The meadow looks like a sticker**: hard-edged flat sage shapes lying on the dirt like rugs, flowers sprinkled evenly at one height. Fix: an ash → sprouts → grass edge band, slightly raised ground, drifts, mixed heights, a grow-in animation.
   - **Done.** The ground shader paints a band from ash to dark turned soil with seedlings, to patchy young grass, to a combed sward in drifts; the turf is raised 7 cm; seedling models come up where the meadow borders ash, then tufts and tall seeding grass, then the flowers open (yarrow added; heights now range from sprouts to grass seed heads), each on its own delay.
4. **The ending**: flat noisy olive terrain, a grey stream that reads as tarmac, hills of evenly scattered pink lollipop trees (far_oak is a hexagonal block on a stick); no Elder stump, no village, no warm sun; "398 cells" is developer wording. Fix: a low shot past the keeper toward a stump with a sapling, village roofs with chimney smoke, a low warm sun with long shadows, a stream with banks, trees in groves, "acres" not "cells".
   - **Done.** After the sweep the camera comes down low behind the keeper, who stands by the Elder's charred stump with a green sapling growing out of it, the village roofs and every chimney's smoke beyond; a low east sun throws long shadows (shadow range 90 m for the shot); water shades shallow to deep with a pale bank line and mirrors the dawn, with wet banks and gravel; the hills are planted in groves of rebuilt carved oaks that turn green once the curse lifts; the letter counts acres. HUD hidden throughout.
5. **Forest interior**: half of each frame near-black; hex oaks are one purple cushion copied hundreds of times with the same lavender rim and no trunks; a black blob in the middle of the grove-fire shot. Fix: lift shadows to L* 20-25, lighter trails, 3-4 canopy shapes with the drooping points the art bible promises, colour variation per tree, visible trunks.
   - **Done.** A flat night fill weighted toward dark colours lifts the woods at 844x390 to L* 16-19 in the darkest quarter of the frame and 21-29 at the median (measured on the phone screenshots; was 5-6 and 7) without washing out plaster or earth; ground colours lifted and trails lighter; four oak silhouettes (round, tall, leaning, weeping) with long bare trunks and hanging points; every tree gets its own plum, blue or grey cast and rim strength; burning ground is char.
6. **Village models**: the lodge is a stacked-ring cone on a cylinder; both cottages are the same model and doghouse-sized next to the keeper; the well is a tub; the woodpile a pile of balls; flagstones evenly scattered blobs on untextured dirt. Fix: cottages 1.5-2x bigger with different roofs, eaves and chimneys; a proper path with partly sunk stones; painted dirt and moss.
   - **Done.** Three cottages at house size (thatch with a fieldstone stack, wooden shingles with a brick chimney, hipped slate with a clay pot), each smoking; a timber-framed lodge on a coursed stone ground floor with a jettied upper floor, dormer, porch and great gable chimney; a coursed fieldstone well with a shingled roof, windlass and bucket; split-log woodpile under a plank roof; flagstones flatter and sunk at random depths; the earth painted in warm and cool patches, worn round the well, pebbles and moss.
7. **The notebook is still a shop table**: identical rows on every page, six same-size tabs, the same footer on every mostly-empty left page, text ignoring the ruled lines, an even-vignette scorched edge, the margin note cut by the gutter. Fix: a different treatment per page (tool sketches, tally marks for upgrade levels, pasted-in helper portraits, a burn map on the left page), text on the ruled lines, uneven tabs.
   - **Done.** The left page holds a burn map drawn from the real forest (hatched ash, red fire dots, the river, the rings, the Elder or its sprout, an x for the keeper) and a doodle per chapter; tools are big sketches, upgrades are tally marks with pencil dots for what's left, helpers are photos taped in, free magic is ringed in ink; every line of writing sits on a rule (rows are whole lines tall and the rules scroll with them); tabs are cut to different heights and depths; margin notes sit under the tags, clear of the gutter.
8. **Numbers**: IM Fell's old-style figures leak in ("+11" reads "+II", "x1" reads "XI"); a title wraps with "minutes" orphaned; HUD second lines ~7 px at 844x390. Fix: lining figures for every number; at least 11-12 px text on phones.
   - **Done.** Keeper Fell (IM Fell English SC with thickened Libre Caslon lining figures, built by `art/fonts/make_keeper_fell.py`, OFL) replaces IM Fell, and Alegreya Sans sets `lnum` everywhere; floating numbers use the tabular counter face at a constant screen size. Phones get a 1.32x UI scale (the smallest type lands at 12 px on 844x390) while the thumb controls keep their size; wrapped notes and entries never leave a lone last word.
9. **Offers**: the parcel is flat isometric clip-art in a style used nowhere else; the tag is a plain rectangle on a dead-straight line. Fix: redraw the parcel in the ink style; a die-cut tag on sagging twine.
   - **Done.** The parcel is a soft paper bundle drawn with wobbling ink lines, hatched shading, tucked folds, a scorch, twine both ways with a bow and loose frayed ends, and a wax seal. The offer tag is a die-cut shipping tag (clipped corners, reinforced hole, scorched foot) hanging from twine that sags and sways.
10. **Mint hearts read as Valentine health pickups; the Elder Tree never reads as a landmark.** Fix: a knot or seed pod with a mint glow; a skyline silhouette for the Elder visible from the village.
   - **Done.** The hearts are now seed pods split open round a glowing mint seed (in the giants, the Elder and the freed-critter icon). The Elder's finger limbs reach much higher, it holds a glowing seed up in its palm, and a big pale storybook moon sits behind its crown as seen from the village, so it reads as a black hand on the skyline.

## Keep

- The charred-plank HUD, the log-slice touch buttons, the taped notes with a drawn finger.
- The notebook details and voice: ribbon close, KEPT / IN HAND stamps on tags, margin notes, the icon set, the dry lines.
- The opening village shots: hooked pines, the giant tree on the skyline, warm lanterns against moonlit plum. The flame drawing itself is good; the problem is how many flames and how big.

**Kept:** the plank HUD, log-slice buttons and taped notes are unchanged; the notebook keeps its ribbon, stamps, tags, margin notes, icons and voice; the village keeps its lanterns and hooked pines; the new bonfire and grass-front drawings are drawn by the same flipbook code in the same inked style. Phone readability (lighter interior, lining figures, 12 px type, fire that can't bury the keeper or the HUD) is in items 1, 5 and 8.
