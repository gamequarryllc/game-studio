# Art review 1: fresh-eyes critic, 2026-11-09 (first build)

Reviewed all 18 screenshots and the model sheet against the studio's "Look and feel" standard. The next pass works from this list. Mark each item done here when fixed, then get a second review.

**Art pass 2 (2026-11-09):** all 12 items below are marked done with what changed. The "Keep" list was kept (kraft tags, tape, price tags, in-world copy, felt-tip windows and labels, the castle's marker bricks, the truss ceiling, the ink-outline icons, the red bottle-cap stick). Needs the second fresh-eyes review.

## Problems, worst first

1. **Fire looks like clip-art**: every flame is the same cream-cored teardrop, same size, same upright angle; up close flames fill the screen; burning boxes get a pasted lava-noise texture; no smoke. Fix: 5-6 different cut-paper flame shapes with torn edges, a darker red base (#b8321c), one tall flame per building leaning with the wind; fade flames within ~3 m of the camera; grey tissue-paper smoke drifting with the fans; burning as a dark-brown scorch spreading across the box with a thin glowing edge, not magma.
   - **Done:** six cut-paper flame shapes with torn edges and a #b8321c base (`art/textures/make_fire_sheets.py` -> `models/flames.png`), one tall lead flame per building leaning hardest with the fans, stop-motion flips at 8 fps, dithered fade inside ~3 m, torn grey tissue smoke drifting downwind (`models/smoke.png`); burning is a single wavy dark-brown scorch front climbing from the floor with a thin glowing edge (no noise emission).
2. **Burnt-out buildings are black holes**: pure-black slabs and splat decals; a fallen tower is a stiff black cube. Fix: 3-4 crumpled or folded Blender states per building type (curled flaps, exposed corrugation), grey ash piles (#8a8580); char #2a2420, never #000; embers cool to grey.
   - **Done:** 16 Blender burnt states (`art/blender/rubble.py`): 4 crumpled houses, 3 fallen offices and 3 fallen copy-paper towers lying along their fall line, a toppled turret, 3 castle heaps, 2 small heaps; curled flaps, exposed flutes, torn holes, grey ash, char #2a2420 with a sheen; embers glow in the folds and cool to grey over ~25 s (`shaders/rubble.gdshader`).
3. **Every model uses the same material**: plain orange kraft with baked shading, windows in a perfect grid, stock low-poly trees, flat lime grass. Fix: a real recycled-cardboard city: printed shipping boxes (stencil marks, barcodes, this-way-up arrows, made-up brands), inside-out cereal boxes, egg-carton roofs, toilet-roll turrets, straw antennas, visible packing tape and cut edges; trees of crumpled tissue on pencils; lawns of green construction paper; varied window spacing with gaps.
   - **Done:** districts rebuilt from recycling: painted cereal boxes, milk/juice cartons, a tissue box, an inside-out box (suburbs, `suburbs.py`); printed shipping boxes with made-up brands, stencils, barcodes, certificate stamps (downtown, `downtown.py`); stacked white copy-paper boxes with lids (skyscrapers, `skyline.py`); egg boxes and toilet/kitchen rolls (castle); tissue-on-pencil trees; construction-paper lawns; window rows with gaps.
4. **Held tools are primitives with no hand**: the magnifier is a white disc that neither magnifies nor makes a hot spot; the blowtorch is a blue cylinder; the rocket reads as a bazooka. Fix: a chunky giant hand holding each tool. Magnifier: ridged brass rim, turned wooden handle, a lens that distorts what's behind it, a bright focus dot with a wisp of smoke. Blowtorch: gas canister with a domed top, valve knob, brass nozzle. Rocket: a paper rocket on a stick fired from a glass bottle.
   - **Done:** a chunky drawn hand with an ink outline holds every tool (`tools.py`): magnifier with a turned handle, ridged brass rim and a lens that really magnifies (a small camera looks through it) plus a focus dot with smoke; a dripping candle; a blue gas canister with domed top, valve knob, brass nozzle and a blue jet; a glass bottle with a paper rocket on a stick.
5. **Flat, pale lighting**: almost everything between 60% and 85% brightness, no warm/cool contrast, fire lights nothing. Fix: ambient down ~35%, a warm key (#ffe2b0) from one skylight with long shadows, a cool fill (#7f93b0), 2-4 shared fire lights, haze that darkens as the city burns.
   - **Done:** ambient 0.62 -> 0.4 (#7f93b0), warm #ffe2b0 key at 28 degrees with long shadows (real on computers, baked along the key on phones), cool #7f93b0 fill, four pooled fire lights computed in the shaders (phones too), haze that browns and thickens as the city burns.
6. **No layout or sense of scale**: an even carpet of same-height boxes; the first screen shows no warehouse; a wide FOV makes edge towers lean. Fix: FOV ~60°, skyscrapers 2-3x downtown height, floor gaps between districts with giant warehouse props (pallet, tape roll, paint can, mug), the castle on a pallet on the main sightline.
   - **Done:** FOV 60; skyscrapers 5.6-6.4 m vs 2-2.75 m downtown; bare-floor gaps between districts with the builder's giant paint cans, mugs, tape rolls, pallet, pen and scissors; the castle on a pallet straight up the main street (the block in front kept low); the first screen from the warehouse door shows trusses, skylights and the castle.
7. **Too much UI, desktop wording**: up to 12 UI pieces at once; "+371 +60" prints over the CHAIN stamp; "Hold the left mouse button", "B to watch", "Click or press G". Fix: one find note at a time, spaced popups, device-aware prompts (touch wording on phones).
   - **Done:** one note at a time (finds replace each other), chain stamps clear floating numbers, one floating number at a time, "Too far" only while burning, touch wording on phones and no click / press G / B to watch / left mouse button anywhere.
8. **The shop box is a flat panel**: 8 equal tabs (a "Shop" tab inside the shop, Settings and Dev with the same gear), a template 2x2 card grid, a broken purchases layout (floating "Coins" header, empty gap). Fix: draw the inside of the box in perspective with darker inner walls and a corrugated rim, 5 divider tabs of different widths, feature the next affordable tool, hide Dev.
   - **Done:** the inside of a box in perspective (darker inner walls, corrugated cut rim, flaps), five tabs cut to different widths (Tools, Tricks, Helpers, Extras, Finds), the next tool propped up front as a big card, rows as crooked paper strips (no card grid), settings behind a dial, Dev off the tabs (F1 or hold the box's name, playtest only).
9. **The starter popup is a stock modal**: centred, two stacked full-width buttons, a gift icon on "A box of matches". Fix: a real matchbox whose drawer slides open, the price on a tag tied with string, "not now" as a small scribble.
   - **Done:** a printed matchbox whose drawer slides out (matches, a bundle of coins), the price on a tag tied to the drawer with string, "not now" scribbled underneath.
10. **Permanent Marker everywhere** (the default free "handwritten" font). Fix: a stencil display font such as Saira Stencil One (OFL) for district names and headers, like real box markings; keep the marker font only for numbers and the chain stamp.
   - **Done:** Saira Stencil One (OFL, licence in `fonts/`) for headers, tabs, titles and button words; Permanent Marker only for numbers, prices and the chain stamp; Patrick Hand for notes.
11. **Touch controls don't match; the wind tag reads as a phone signal meter.** Fix: all three as bottle caps or card discs; wind as a ribbon on a fan whose length shows strength.
   - **Done:** all three phone controls are bottle caps (red stick cap on a card disc, a big orange fire cap with a flame, a blue jump cap); wind is a marker-drawn fan with a red ribbon streaming downwind, longer and livelier as it blows harder.
12. **Rockets show no rocket**, only a faint glow. Fix: a spinning paper rocket with a spark trail and a felt-tip-coloured burst.
   - **Done:** paper rockets on sticks fly from the bottle, spinning, growing from hand size, with a spark trail; bursts throw felt-tip-coloured stars and sparks.

## Keep

- The kraft tags, masking tape and price-tag buttons, and the in-world copy voice ("A lost sock.", "Too far. Walk closer").
- The open-flapped boxes with felt-tip windows and labels (KITCHEN, BANK, LOFTS) and the castle's marker brickwork.
- The truss-and-skylight warehouse ceiling, the consistent ink-outline icons, the red bottle-cap joystick.

## Direction for the next pass

A kid's craft project built from recycling, lit by late-afternoon skylight in a dim warehouse. Each district has its own materials: suburbs from painted cereal boxes and tissue trees, downtown from printed shipping boxes, skyscrapers from white printer-paper boxes, the castle from egg cartons and toilet rolls on a pallet. Lower ambient, one warm key with long shadows, cool blue in the shade; fire is the brightest, warmest thing on screen and lights what's near it. Fire is cut paper with grey tissue smoke; burning creeps across the board as a glowing edge and leaves crumpled shells and grey ash, never black voids. Every tool in a drawn giant hand. Giant warehouse props between districts for scale. UI: stencil headers, one popup at a time, touch-only wording, the shop and offers as physical objects (a box with depth, a matchbox, luggage tags on string).

# Art review 2: second fresh-eyes critic, 2026-11-09 (after review-1 fixes + QA)

**Verdict: Almost.** The UI and the downtown boxes come from a specific vision; the fire, the burnt aftermath, the castle, the trees and the held tools still read as a prototype with an asset pack. **Phone readability:** HUD text is legible, but the floor is too bright for good contrast, hint text and floating numbers are too small at 844x390, and the bottom-right corner (hand, JUMP, HOLD) plus a permanent offer tag are cluttered. This review is the polish list toward "ready to play".

## Problems, worst first

1. **The burnt aftermath looks generated**: the floor scorch is a noise texture with white blotches (reads as camouflage or a broken texture); rubble is a near-black slab that reads as a hole. Fix: 6-8 hand-painted scorch decals with a soot fade outward from each footprint (char brown to ash grey); light ash on rubble tops, curled card edges catching the key light, embers that cool; one wall left standing so the building stays recognisable.
2. **The fire is still weak**: burnt blocks covered in small identical flame spikes like a pin cushion; flames floating off facade sides; no smoke column; sparks as white blurry blobs; no light cast on neighbours. Fix: one big flame cluster per building rooted at edges and openings; fewer, larger flames on rubble; torn-tissue smoke leaning with the fans; orange-speck sparks; a warm light pool on the floor and nearby boxes.
3. **Lighting and floor look like a greybox**: almost-white flat empty concrete, blown-out skylights, no late-afternoon warmth, no value contrast between kraft and floor. Fix: floor ~30% darker with joints, tape lines and stains; a warmer key; a darker foreground; edge dressing with craft props (glue gun, scissors, paint pots, offcuts, a pallet, a stepladder) for scale and story.
4. **The castle is primitives**: smooth plastic red and blue cones on plain cylinders, battlements like Duplo studs. Fix: toilet-roll towers with the spiral seam visible, cones rolled from printed card with a taped overlap seam, battlements cut from grey egg-carton cups, the pallet base visible.
5. **Trees and lawns look like a stock asset pack**: low-poly lollipops and stacked-cone pines; flat saturated green lawn planes. Fix: crumpled-tissue trees on six-sided pencils stuck in clay; construction-paper lawns with fibre texture, cut edges and lifted corners, green pulled toward the palette.
6. **Held tools and hand are primitives**: candle, blowtorch and bottle are plain cylinders; the blowtorch has no flame jet; in the rocket shot the hand points at the bottle instead of holding it; the JUMP cap sits on top of the hand. Fix: a grip pose per tool with the thumb wrapped round; a blowtorch with valve knob, label and a roaring blue flame cone; the hand framed above the button cluster.
7. **The shop is a template list**: identical rows (icon, title, text, price on the right); tab icons (thermometer, duck, TV) are clip-art stand-ins for emoji; the "box" is flaps on a flat panel; low-contrast cream-on-brown captions. Fix: tools laid in cut-out inserts of different sizes with the next tool shown big; tab icons as objects from the game's world; higher caption contrast.
8. **HUD loose ends**: the fan tag is mostly blank and the wind ribbon barely visible; an unlabelled tick on the district bar; the hint note overlaps the fan tag; an ad tag stays up permanently; "light something" reads like placeholder text; the CHAIN stamp lingers and jumps x5 → x8 → x5; floating "+2.7" numbers too small. Fix: label the tick, one note at a time, the offer tag only now and then, animate the stamp out, readable numbers.
9. **Repetition**: every downtown box the same kraft with the same blue and yellow window scribbles; identical skyscraper window grids; suburb houses read as plain coloured cubes at play distance. Fix: vary box stock (white, dark brown, printed, faded) and window sets per building; bigger roof prints.
10. **The ending isn't its own moment**: hand and bottle still in frame, a castle turret still standing, the noise scorch visible. Fix: hide the hand; a composed slow camera move over the ashes.

## Keep

- The downtown shipping-box models: sagging flaps, tape, made-up brands (Kestrel, Mossy, No. 9, Paperlark), felt-tip windows.
- The UI style and copy: taped cut-card tags, bottle-cap buttons, stencil headers, the matchbox starter offer, the delivery-note ending, lines like "Office box: too thick, needs the candle."
- The castle sightline at the end of the main street with light shafts through the trusses, and the paper cut-out people.
