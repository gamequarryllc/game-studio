# Art review 1: fresh-eyes critic, 2026-11-09 (first build)

Status: all 12 items fixed in art pass 2 (notes under each). Next: a second fresh-eyes review.

Reviewed every screenshot, the model contact sheet and the icon sheet against the studio's "Look and feel" standard. The next pass works from this list. Mark each item done here when fixed, then get a second review.

## Problems, worst first

1. **The underground is one flat orange-brown wash**: no darks; rock facets read as crumpled paper; pebbles are flat confetti quads floating in the air; ore is flat teal triangles. Fix: lamp light falls off to a zone-tinted soot (clay #2A1610) by 8 m; strata bands every 0.5-1.5 m, each about 15% lighter or darker than the next; a 256px hand-painted triplanar rock texture; half-buried 3D pebbles; ore as nuggets standing out of the wall with a rim glint.
   **Done.** Headlamp is now the main light (spot 4.0, gentle decay) over a dim fill and lantern; depth fog clear for 1.6 m and the zone's own near-black by the lamp's reach (~9 m); new painted rock texture (knife-laid value patches, chipped facets lit from above, sideways bedding strokes, sparse jagged joints) at two scales; strata bands 12-22% apart with warm/cool shifts, seams and grit beds; stronger facets; darker damp 3D pebbles; ore nuggets with rim glint.
2. **Clay and Fossil Bed shots are the same image**: a full-screen yellow dust card plus a white wedge (the drill bit clipping the camera near plane). Fix: viewmodel on its own camera; fade dust near the lens; each zone gets its own hue and a prop.
   **Done.** Viewmodel on its own camera (never clips), dust fades out within 1.3 m of the lens; every layer has its own fog tint and a lit landmark (timbers, mine cart, ribcages, crystals, lava, basalt, core spires), now with real materials (lava shader, faceted crystal shader, white-hot spires).
3. **The surface looks like stock Godot**: lime #5CC232 noise grass, default sky, mint fog hills, flat noon light. Fix: late-afternoon sun, warm key #FFD39A, cool ambient #8EA6C8, long shadows; olive grass #7E9A4C with darker clumps, worn umber paths, a spoil heap, hills in three values fading with distance.
   **Done.** Late-afternoon sun (#FFD39A key, cool #8EA6C8 fill), long shadows, olive grass with painted clumps, worn umber paths, a spoil heap, and depth haze that fades the hills in steps; the tool in your hands is now lit by the sun too.
4. **Spawn composition**: you spawn facing a headframe leg, the shack is hidden, an endless fence cuts the frame. Fix: spawn on a 3/4 view, shack on the left, headframe on the right, a path leading in.
   **Done.** You spawn on the path in at a 3/4 view: shack on the left, headframe on the right, the sign and fence gap in the middle distance.
5. **Hazard-stripe bollards** read as a modern car park (wrong era) and their random lean looks broken. Fix: weathered timber posts, sagging rope, one enamel "Open shaft" sign.
   **Done.** Weathered timber posts with a sagging rope and one cream enamel "Open shaft" sign facing the path in (its face catches the sky so it reads against the low sun).
6. **Primitive-soup models**: mole and robo-mole are eggs; oak is icospheres and pine is stacked cones; the barrel is a bucket with hoops floating off it; the crate is a plain bevelled box; the jackhammer looks like a syringe; the borer is a salmon drum with no cutting head; the pickaxe has one point; tools drift to salmon #E8956B (not in the art bible). Fix: pear-bodied mole with spade claws, snout and hard hat; brass-boiler robo-mole; bulging barrel; planked crate; oversized double-ended pick; T-handle jackhammer; toothed cone on the borer; vertex AO and worn edges on every model.
   **Done.** Robo-mole rebuilt as a brass steam tunnelling engine on caterpillar tracks with a fluted drill cone and scoops; mole has a long pale snout, big nose and whiskers; oak is a gnarled forked trunk with separate faceted clumps; pine is whorls of drooping boughs; bulging stave barrel and planked crate; one-piece forged double-ended pick; jackhammer is a blackened iron rock drill with brass bands (no hazard stripes); borer has a toothed cone; AO and edge wear baked on every model, palette colours only.
7. **The headframe reads as a steel radio mast.** Fix: heavy squared timbers, back legs sloping out, a bigger sheave wheel, a cable that reaches the cage.
   **Done.** Squat, broad tower of heavy squared timbers (0.3 m posts), back legs raked far out, X-braces, a ladder, a bigger 1.6 m sheave, and a cable down to the cage.
8. **Template UI**: every element is the same chamfered plate with four corner rivets and the same tick scratches; the shop is identical rows under five equal tabs. Fix: material by role (brass for money, chipped enamel for signs, paper ledger cards with stamped prices for items), uneven tabs, rivets only on brass.
   **Done.** Material by role: brass for money and the shop header (the only plates with rivets, enforced in UiTheme), cream enamel for signs and hints, paper ledger cards with stamped prices for items, manila file tabs of uneven width.
9. **Off-palette blue (#4F86BF)** on the ad buttons, the rare-find frame and Jump is the loudest colour on screen. Fix: ad buttons in verdigris enamel or as a cream ticket with a red play stamp; Jump in soot iron.
   **Done.** No blue left: ad buttons are cream tickets with a red play stamp, Jump is soot iron with a brass rim, rarity colours are palette tints.
10. **HUD overlaps**: the find card covers the gauge labels and stays up during the lift ride; zone names float in tiny grey lowercase; two 2x offers show at once; Jump overlaps Dig; the Down button has a soft glow halo.
   **Done.** Find card lives in the right column and hides during the lift ride; one 2x offer at a time; Jump clear of Dig; no glow halo; zone tags are cream labels in sentence case; the right column now grows leftward so the offer tag never runs off a phone screen; touch controls hide the same frame a menu, ad or ride starts; floating money numbers keep one size on screen.
11. **Viewmodels**: the shovel is held like a broom; the drill is hidden behind the buttons. Fix: a gloved hand, with the tool's working end framed above the buttons.
   **Done.** New gloves built from smooth tubes (curled fingers, thumb over them, flared gauntlet cuff, sleeve) on every tool; the working ends sit above the touch buttons; the shovel blade is bigger and lit.
12. **The lift ride is a yellow void**, and finds are 2D billboards (make them Blender models).
   **Done.** The shaft is wider and dressed for the ride: each layer's own rock colour and strata streaming past, timber sets, lamps flashing by, layer signs, each layer's wall pieces (roots, shells, bones, crystals, magma vents) and its landmark in a niche with its light; the view looks up or down the shaft. All 16 finds are Blender models (ammonite, amber cabochon, cracked egg, keeled dragon scale and a cutaway-Earth core rebuilt this pass).

## Keep

- The Rye and Zilla Slab fonts, the brass "Supply Shack" header and the money plate.
- The writing voice ("Grandpa's old shovel. Good for dirt, hopeless on rock.").
- The icon set's thick outline and flat fills, and the crooked paper offer tag.

## Direction for the next pass

Fix lighting first. Underground is a warm amber pool that fades to near-black within 8 m; each zone gets its own dark tint and one landmark (fossil ribs, crystals, lava as a second red light), with strata bands on every wall. The surface moves to late afternoon with a composed spawn view. Rebuild the weak models up to the standard of the shack (the best model): oversized working ends, AO and edge wear, only the seven palette colours. Drop the blue and the rivet-on-every-corner plate; each UI element's material follows its job. Fix the dust and the near-plane clip before the next screenshots.

# Art review 2: second fresh-eyes critic, 2026-11-09 (after fixes + QA)

**Verdict: No.** The UI and the surface pass as indie work; the underground, where players spend ~90% of their time, still reads as a game-jam prototype. The next pass is about the underground.

Status: all 9 items fixed in art round 3 (notes under each). Next: a third fresh-eyes review.

## Problems, worst first

1. **Underground is muddy and samey**: each layer is one colour wash inside a hard-edged headlamp circle; the same dried-mud crack texture on every layer; flat white flecks scattered like confetti; the lava is a banned noise-blotch texture under a full-screen red wash. Fix: each layer its own material with strata bands and two rock values; a soft lamp falloff with a cool bounce light; flecks become real pebbles or go; lava as a dark crust with glowing cracks that lights the walls.
   **Done.** Each layer has its own hand-painted texture (a texture array: crumbly soil with root hairs, smeared laminated clay, jointed granite, cross-bedded shelly sandstone, a cobble conglomerate, faceted crystal rock with veins, vesicular basalt, folded peridotite, streaming iron, core crystal with molten channels), its own strata thickness and a second rock in alternate beds, pebbles lying in beds in the layer's own stone (no pale flecks); a wide, soft headlamp with a cool bounce near you; lava is black crust plates rafting on a glowing melt with glowing cracks in the walls and an orange light on them, the fog near-black.
2. **Floating text looks broken**: "Coal +96", "Lift", "Fossil Bone +8K" pop up huge in grey Rye, overlapping and drawn through rock; the "Lift up" sign's letters spill off the board; debug-style labels under the crosshair ("Dirt", "Sandstone · 13 hits"); red "too hard" text on a red scene. Fix: small Zilla Slab popups that don't draw through rock and merge per tick; sign lettering painted into the texture; a cracked-rock icon instead of "too hard".
   **Done.** Money pops in small Zilla Slab numbers in the colour of what you dug, hidden by rock in front (depth tested, set out from the wall), the newest one counting up while you keep digging; sign lettering is painted into enamel textures (lift stop, "Open shaft", the shack's board, the headframe plate, the layer plates in the shaft); no labels under the crosshair, just a cracked-rock mark (or a riveted plate for the shaft wall) when your tool can't touch it.
3. **Arms and held tools look amateur**: arms are stacks of orange rings or a plain brown drainpipe; the pickaxe is one smooth gold material; the shovel blade is a flat grey card; the laser drill reads as a flashlight. Fix: a real sleeve with cuff and folds, gloves with knuckles; worn steel edges, wood grain, grip tape; a distinct silhouette per tool.
   **Done.** Gloves with knuckles, finger joints, stitched seams and a strapped wrist; work-shirt sleeves bunched in folds with a turned-back cuff, seam and button; hickory and ash handles with grain, cloth grip tape wound on the pick, steel cooled so it stays steel under the lamp, forged-black heads worn bright at the edges; the laser drill is now a pistol-gripped coil gun (copper coil, glass capacitor, pressure gauge, cooling fins, three-pronged emitter), nothing like a torch.
4. **The shop is a template**: dark modal, five equal tabs, identical rows, a red Remove Ads banner on every tab plus a second buy button for it, "(test)" on prices, the finds grid as identical "?" cards with unreadable grey-on-tan labels. Fix: build it as the shack counter: tools on a pegboard with paper tags, upgrades in a ledger, finds in a drawer cabinet, Remove Ads as one enamel sign.
   **Done.** The shop is the shack: its plank back wall, tools hanging on a pegboard with kraft paper price tags tied on (and a note pinned below about the next tool), upgrades in an open ledger with levels kept in pencil tally marks, "hands wanted" notices for the crew, the finds in a cabinet of drawers with brass label holders and readable cards, and No ads + 2x as one enamel sign on the bonus wall (sold nowhere else, no duplicate button, store prices with no "(test)"). The five sections are five different objects, not equal tabs.
5. **Underground props are primitives**: the mantle is plain hex prisms in flat colours; the timber set is two bare sticks; the mine cart looks like a nightstand (no wheels, no rails); ribs identical and evenly spaced like a comb, kinking at joints; crystals matte and unlit; the inner core washed-out beige. Fix: varied, broken, half-buried ribs; timbers with a cap beam and wedges; a cart on rails; glowing crystals; the core as molten amber, the brightest moment in the game.
   **Done.** Rebuilt in Blender: a skeleton weathering out of an ochre mound (vertebrae of every size, a few knocked out of line, ribs whole, snapped, collapsed and loose, a femur and a jaw); a fossil slab with ammonites and a fish for the Fossil Bed; a timber set with a cap beam lapped over splayed posts, driven wedges, lagging, a bowed split post and the lantern on its hook; a riveted ore tub on flanged wheels on rails and sleepers; pillowed, split, glowing mantle columns; quartz with proper terminations that glows from inside; the inner core as a molten amber pool crowned with white-hot crystals and the strongest light in the game.
6. **Surface reads as an asset pack**: acacia savanna trees in a temperate field; a blurry ground texture; the first dig is a dark smudge with no grass lip or dirt pile. Fix: maples, oaks and birch; a worn path to the headframe; clods and a spoil heap around the hole.
   **Done.** Oaks, maples (one side turning amber) and birches (white bark, black marks) built in Blender as single lobed crowns on branching trunks, mixed with a few pines; a worn path to the headframe plus the miners' short cut from the east gap; digging the field throws clods and turf onto the grass and heaps the first hole's dirt on a spoil heap beside it (it grows as you dig and is saved).
7. **HUD crowded on a phone**: plates in all four corners stacking down the middle; at 844x390 some text is ~6 px; two depth bars side by side; the ad ticket never leaves. Fix: merge the bars into one brass tube; the layer banner has its moment then fades; the ad ticket only when relevant.
   **Done.** One brass tube for the whole planet (layer bands, finds, lift stop, deepest mark, the needle) under a readout plate with the depth and the layer's name; the layer banner lands, holds 3.4 s with the stage to itself (other toasts wait), then lifts away; the 2x ticket shows only while a boost runs or for ~30 s at a moment it's worth having (a new layer, a long chip at slow rock), never with Remove Ads; every word is at least 20 px at 1280 x 720 (11 px on an 844 x 390 phone), checked in the smoke test.
8. **Repetitive copy**: every layer banner says "Harder rock, worth N.Nx more" with jumpy numbers. Fix: one in-world line per layer.
   **Done.** Each layer has its own line in the fiction, no numbers ("Shells in every bed. This was the sea floor once.", "Molten iron. Your boots were not rated for this.").
9. **Smaller things**: desktop prompts in most shots ("Click... left mouse button", "E: ride the lift"); the red-on-black "Open shaft" sign is unreadable; the crew shot shows no crew; the full HUD stays up during the lift ride, where the cage is a few dark sticks.
   **Done.** Phone first: the game starts in touch mode and wording ("tap", "hold Dig") until a mouse click or a walking key; keyboard prompts show the key on a cream keycap ("Ride the lift up to the shack"), never "E:"; the "Open shaft" sign is painted soot and red on cream enamel and glows a little in its shade; the crew shot shows the moles at work round the lift stop; the HUD steps aside for the whole lift ride, inside a rebuilt cage (angle-iron frame, woven mesh walls, folding gate, chequer floor, grab rail, pitched bonnet with chains).

## Keep

- The UI's look: brass and iron plates, Rye with Zilla Slab, the crooked enamel offer tag, ticket-stub ad buttons, ledger-paper rows.
- The finds: their writing and the odd, specific find models (cassette, subway car, satellite, mosquito in amber, mole, robo-mole).
- The surface layout: shack left, headframe right, rope fences leading the eye, a low warm sun with long shadows; the shack and headframe models.
