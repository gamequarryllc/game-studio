# Plainproof (home inventory for insurance): progress

Kept current after every finished item, so a run that is cut off can be picked up from here.

## Status: 1.0.1 being readied for the App Store (2026-11-21)

**Live:** version 1.0.0 (build 33), submitted 2026-11-12, without the claim features. **In TestFlight:** 1.0.1 (69), playtest flavor, with everything below; the owner is testing it on his iPhone with a list from the 2026-11-21 thread. **Readied for 1.0.1:** the listing at version 1.0.1 with its "What's New" text, six screenshots (a claim screen added), Pro at $9.99, a release build asked for (`testflight-request.json`), and `tools/asc_listing.py` able to make the new version and write "What's New". **Left for the owner:** testing 69, then Submit for Review in App Store Connect.

## Done

- **Several claims at once (2026-11-16, the owner's request before trying build 64).** A place can have up to 12 claims open, one per loss (a break-in and a burst pipe are two claims, often with two adjusters), each with its own name, claim number, date, cause, description, items and package. Claims are listed on the Report tab ("Start another claim" adds one; the first still opens with the explanation); the Rooms tab says "2 claims open" and leads there. The picker for a claim says when an item is already in another claim, and it can still be added (damaged in one loss, stolen in another); an item's screen shows each claim it is in with its own status and note, and "Add to another claim" asks which. The schedule, spreadsheet and package are per claim, and their file names carry the claim's number or name. Stored as `doc.claims` with each claim holding its items (`{itemId, loss, note}`); a save or backup from build 64 (one `claim`, the status on each item) comes back as one claim. Deleting an item or a room takes it out of every claim. Also fixed: the package's read-me listed a Videos folder when only other rooms had videos. 168 unit tests and the browser run (30 steps, a new one with two claims at once) pass.
- **Claim features (2026-11-15, the owner picked all eight ideas from the audit and added policy upload; branch `plainproof-claim-features`, stacked on the fixes).** **Claim mode** (Report tab, Make a claim): mark items stolen, damaged, destroyed or lost (one at a time from the item, or a whole room at once), the claim number, date of loss, cause and what happened, a note per item; the schedule of loss is the report limited to the claimed items with a status on each and the claim details on the cover; a claim spreadsheet; End the claim clears the marks and keeps the items. **Claim package**: one zip for the adjuster (`src/domain/claimPackage.ts`): the schedule, the spreadsheet, a read-me, a numbered folder per item with every photo and receipt, the walk-through videos of the rooms involved and the policy documents. **Policy**: a PDF or photos of the pages kept with the place (`documents/plainproof/files/`), the contents limit, deductible and special limits, and a coverage check (worth more than the limit, close to it, single items over a category limit or over $1,500 that may need scheduling). **Walk-through videos** per room (up to 500 MB each, recorded or chosen, played full screen with expo-video), in the report as a line per room. **Scanning**: read a serial number off a label and the date, shop and price off a receipt, on the phone with Apple's Vision (the app's own native module, `modules/plainproof-native`). **Warranty date** on items, in the report and the spreadsheet. **Reminders** (local notifications, off by default): once a year, when the backup is behind, a month before a warranty ends. **iCloud Drive backup** (off by default): the person picks a folder with the system folder picker; a backup is saved there 90 seconds after the last change and when the app goes to the background, and the one before is kept as "(previous)". Backups now carry videos and documents (format 2 still; older backups restore as before). Checks: 162 unit tests, lint, typecheck, the web export and the iPhone bundle, and the browser run (29 steps: new steps for the warranty, a claim end to end with the schedule, spreadsheet and package unzipped and checked, the policy and coverage check, a video added and played, the backup holding the video and the PDF, and the settings). **Still needs a phone:** all of the native module (it has not been compiled before the CI build), scanning on real labels and receipts, Quick Look for the policy, the folder bookmark surviving a restart, recording a video with sound, and notifications arriving.
- **Claim-readiness fixes (2026-11-15, the owner asked for the audit's fixes before any new features).** From the audit of the report as a claim document: the report shows the replacement value in its own column and marks totals that used the price paid, says under the totals how the total was reached and how many items have no value, prints when each item was recorded and when its earliest photo was taken (photos keep the camera's date from EXIF; the stored copy still has no EXIF), offers every photo full size in an appendix with its dates, and prints the full time with its UTC offset, the app version and a report reference (SHA-256 of the data) on the cover and on every room line. Items have a condition. Purchase dates after today are refused. The spreadsheet keeps serial and model numbers as text (Excel used to drop leading zeros and turn IMEIs into 3.56938E+14), and adds replacement value, price paid, the value basis, the recorded date, the first photo date and the item id. **A data-loss bug is fixed:** a save file that couldn't be read at launch used to be treated as a fresh install, and the first edit then wrote an empty inventory over it; launch now falls back to the spare, keeps a copy of a damaged file, and otherwise shows a recovery screen that saves nothing until a backup is restored or the person chooses to start over. Also: "1.299" means 1,299 (was $1.30) and money fields show the amount as understood; the backup reminder notices every change, not only edited items; a restore says how many photos the backup lacked; the quantity can be typed; the Rooms card counts rooms with something in them, as the report does. 137 unit tests, lint, typecheck, the web export, and the browser run (23 steps: new checks for the recovery screen, the photo appendix and the report's new lines) pass. **Still needs a phone:** whether the iPhone's PDF draws the page foot (the room lines now carry the name and reference either way), the EXIF date from the camera and the photo library, and a report with every photo on a large inventory.
