This policy covers the games Game Quarry makes in its studio: {{games_sentence}}, on iPhone and iPad, and any other Game Quarry game that links to this page. In short: the games have no accounts and keep your progress on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what the ad network needs to show ads and what the App Store needs to sell you something.

What stays on your device

Your save (how far you have got, your tools, finds and settings) is stored only on your device. We don't receive it, and deleting the game deletes it.

The free test versions that run in a web browser keep their save in that browser's storage for this site. Clearing the site's data removes it. Those versions show no real ads and sell nothing.

Ads (Google AdMob)

On iPhone and iPad the games show ads through Google AdMob: video ads you choose to watch for a reward, and an occasional full-screen ad at a natural break. To pick and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent first. You can change your choice at any time in the game's settings, under Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone and iPad the game asks permission before using your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, you still get ads, just less personalised ones.
  • Each game sells a one-time purchase that turns off the full-screen ads. Reward videos are always your choice.

Google's own page explains how it uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Payments are handled entirely by the App Store. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check purchases and to restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase history for that game, and basic device information. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, your email address or any account.
  • We don't run analytics of our own, and we don't sell your personal data.
  • The games aren't aimed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising identifier or switch off tracking in your device's settings (on iPhone: Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking), change your ad consent in the game where it applies, and delete all of a game's data by deleting the game. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, use the links above, or contact us and we'll help.
