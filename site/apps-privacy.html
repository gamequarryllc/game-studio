This policy covers the apps Game Quarry makes in its studio: {{apps_sentence}}, on iPhone, and any other Game Quarry app that links to this page. In short: the apps have no accounts and no servers of ours. What you put in them stays on your iPhone, and we never see it. The only thing that leaves your phone is what the App Store needs to sell you the one-time upgrade and bring it back later.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter (items, photos, receipts, values, notes and settings) is stored only in the app's own storage on your device. It is not uploaded, we don't receive it, and we couldn't read it if we wanted to. Deleting the app deletes it.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or to a computer, that backup may include the app's data, as it does for any app. That copy is yours, under your Apple Account.

The camera and your photos

When you add a picture, the app asks to use the camera, or shows you the system photo picker so it only gets the photos you choose. The pictures are copied into the app's own storage and stay there.

Reports, spreadsheets and backups

A report, a spreadsheet or a backup file is made on your iPhone and handed to the share sheet. Where it goes from there (Files, Mail, AirDrop, a printer, your insurer) is your choice. Once you send a file to someone or save it to a service, their own policy covers it.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Each app is free with one optional purchase, paid once. Payment is handled entirely by the App Store: we never see your card or payment details. The apps use RevenueCat to check that purchase and to restore it on a new iPhone. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID created by the app, the purchase history for that app and basic device information such as the model and iOS version. It receives nothing you have entered in the app. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, your email address or any account.
  • The apps show no ads and contain no advertising or analytics code.
  • We don't sell or share personal data, because we don't have any.
  • The apps are made for adults and are not aimed at children.

The web previews

The test versions on this site run in your browser and keep what you enter in that browser's storage for this site. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing the site's data removes it. They sell nothing.

Your choices

You can delete everything an app holds by deleting the app, or with the erase option where an app has one. For the purchase record held by RevenueCat, contact us and we'll help.
