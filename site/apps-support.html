Every app

Where is my data kept?
On your iPhone only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes what is in it, so make a backup first if you want to keep it.
How do I move to a new iPhone?
Make a backup in the app and save the file somewhere the new phone can reach: iCloud Drive, a computer, or an email to yourself. Install the app on the new iPhone and restore from that file. Where to find both is listed under each app below.
I bought Pro and it's gone.
Use Restore purchase in the app, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Pro is bought once, so it always comes back.
Is there a subscription?
No. Each app is free up to a limit, and one payment removes the limit for good.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
