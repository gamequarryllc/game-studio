Every game

Where is my progress saved?
On your device only. Deleting the game deletes your save, and a save can't be moved to another device.
Do I have to watch ads or buy anything to finish?
No. Every game can be finished without either. Ads and purchases only speed things up.
I bought something and it's gone.
Use Restore purchases in the game (where to find it is listed under each game below). You need to be signed in with the Apple account you bought it with. One-time purchases come back. Packs of in-game currency are used up when you buy them, so they are not restored.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
How do I change my ad choices?
In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game's settings have an Ad privacy choices entry. On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking under Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
